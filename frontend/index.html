How it works

One platform for residents, linemen and administrators.

Duplicate-aware reporting

Reports from the same area, time and issue are linked to one incident. Every resident's report is kept; the affected-user count grows instead of the complaint pile.

Transparent updates

Follow the status, verified cause and repair progress. Official causes are entered only by authorised employees.

Restoration estimates

A machine-learning model estimates restoration time. It is always labelled an estimate; the lineman's confirmed time takes priority.

Live outage map

See active outages near you on a map, with status and affected households.

Lineman workspace

Assigned incidents sorted by priority, with one-tap status, cause and ETA updates.

Admin insight

Area statistics, outage patterns, unusual activity, feedback and a full audit log.

Prototype notice. This is a hackathon prototype. Demo accounts and the historical outage dataset are simulated sample data, not information from any electricity department. Restoration times shown are model estimates.
